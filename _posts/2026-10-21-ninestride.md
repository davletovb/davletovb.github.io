NineStride is an IELTS preparation app covering Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking. It runs as a Telegram Mini App, with a web version, and combines diagnostics, short drills, full tasks, review and AI feedback.

Most preparation apps offer a large bank of practice. The harder question is what a learner should do next, today, in the time they have. NineStride is built around that question.

## The loop

1. **Find the next useful step.** Diagnostics show where the learner is weakest.
2. **Practice with a purpose.** Short drills target that weakness, and full Writing and Speaking tasks test it under realistic conditions.
3. **Review and return.** Review cards and practice history bring an earlier mistake back as a new attempt.

A mechanic earns its place only if it helps the learner work on a real IELTS weakness or measures their performance honestly.

## Feedback needs a standard

Feedback is useful when it points to something the learner can change. A score is a stronger claim and needs stronger evidence. AI-generated band scores for Writing and Speaking have to be calibrated against examiner judgments before they can be trusted, and a working scoring screen is not proof of accuracy. That makes assessment quality, and the quality of the practice content itself, as much a part of the product as the interface.

## Studio

Studio is a second interface on the same backend. Instead of making chat the whole experience, it lays out activities, feedback and progress as a visible path that adapts as the learner works. Chat becomes one tool inside it.

## Where it stands

NineStride is in development. Current work is on content quality, scoring calibration and startup speed inside Telegram.
