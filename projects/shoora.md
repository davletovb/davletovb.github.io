## Purpose

Multiple answers do not automatically make a better answer. This workspace makes the differences between models visible: where they agree, where they disagree, and what a structured critique changes.

The project is now referred to as **Shoora**, while its public repository and README are still named **Conclave**. That naming distinction is intentional until the repository is renamed.

## Workflows

The repository documents independent responses, structured critique, debate, synthesis, custom workflow graphs, persistent runs, and opt-in shared search evidence. Local persistence and provider adapters are implemented, while provider availability depends on installed runtimes and authenticated accounts.

The goal is to make orchestration explicit and inspectable rather than hide a council behind a single answer.
