## Purpose

BandUp brings together IELTS Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking in a more coherent daily learning loop. Diagnostics identify areas to practice; targeted sessions and review connect feedback to what the learner tries next.

The project includes a Telegram Mini App and web experience, with focused drills and larger assessment tasks.

## Studio

Studio is an additional interface direction that shares the existing application backend. It explores structured, adaptive learning instead of treating a chat stream as the whole learning environment.

## Verification boundary

The project remains in development. In particular, AI-generated Writing and Speaking scores require calibration against examiner judgments; a working scoring screen is not evidence of validated assessment accuracy.
